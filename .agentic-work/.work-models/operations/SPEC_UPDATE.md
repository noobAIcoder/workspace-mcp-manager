# Specification Update

## Contract

Specialize the [SDD stage](../profiles/SDD.md) for revisions to existing authority, within the [SDD/TDD lifecycle](../flows/SDD_TDD.md). Inputs include the current specification revision, affected paragraphs, proposed normative deltas, and governed dependencies.

## Revision Responsibilities

Identify changed and preserved requirements, superseded content, affected contracts and acceptance criteria, and required cross-document consequences.

Use the selected specification framework's change areas, propagation graph, document-local authoring rules, and readiness contracts. Record each affected derivation as revised, regenerated, or reviewed unchanged with its reason.

Textual cleanup MUST NOT introduce an unrecorded semantic change. Revision scope MUST follow the normative delta rather than the size of a textual diff.

## Output and Exit

Supply revised candidates and the delta/propagation disposition. Apply SDD's validation, acceptance, no-op, and completion contracts and the lifecycle's blocking and transition rules.
